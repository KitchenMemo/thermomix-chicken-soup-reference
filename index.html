7. Storage and reheating

Refrigerate promptly in shallow containers. Freezer times are quality guidelines for food kept continuously frozen.

Version Fridge (days) Freezer Reheating tip
Chicken and vegetable3–4 days2–3 monthsReheat to 74°C / 165°F.
Noodle soup3–4 days2–3 monthsExpect noodles to soften; assess quality after freezing.
Creamy soup3–4 days2–3 monthsReheat gently and check for separation.
Do not leave the full bowl to cool slowly on the bench. Portion, label with the date, and thaw safely. Perishable food should not sit at room temperature more than 2 hours (1 hour above 32°C / 90°F).

Pre-cook checklist

  • Recipe is marked compatible with your exact model (TM5, TM6 or TM31).
  • Total contents sit below the MAX line, with room for foaming.
  • Correct lid accessory for your model and temperature.
  • Chicken pieces cut evenly; raw chicken kept apart from cooked food.
  • Food thermometer ready: 74°C / 165°F in the thickest piece.
  • Stock label checked before adding any extra salt.
